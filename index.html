Start measurement and place fingertip firmly over the rear camera with torch on. Keep still for 15 seconds.
Algorithm: Camera frames → Red & Green channel mean pixel values extracted. AC component (pulsatile) = peak−trough over each beat window. DC component (baseline) = rolling mean. R-ratio = (AC_red/DC_red) / (AC_green/DC_green) ≈ empirical proxy for SpO₂. SpO₂ estimated via calibration curve: SpO₂ ≈ 110 − 25×R (Beer-Lambert approximation for consumer cameras). Heart rate derived from peak-to-peak interval of red-channel PPG. Perfusion Index = AC/DC × 100%.
